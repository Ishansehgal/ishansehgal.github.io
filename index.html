Welcome to My Robotics Journey

I'm Ishan Sehgal. This interactive space showcases my evolution as a robotics developer. Drive the robot over the colored zones to explore key moments in my story. The yellow line traces the robot's odometry—its traveled path.

The Genesis: First Steps in ROS (B.Tech)

My journey began with a simple Arduino obstacle-avoidance car. I quickly realized I needed more powerful tools for true autonomy. A mentorship with RigBetel Labs introduced me to ROS Noetic, where I modeled a diff-drive robot in URDF and deployed the Nav Stack. This first taste of localization and odometry got me hooked.

The Challenge: e-Yantra Competition (AIR 13)

I led a team in the e-Yantra competition, automating an AMR and a UR5 arm. We moved from simulation to controlling the real robot at IIT Bombay. Securing an All India Rank of 13 was a great achievement, but the real lesson was understanding the vast sim-to-real gap. It solidified my desire to master real-world hardware.

The Build: My Personal ROS2 AMR

Motivated to bridge the sim-to-real gap, I built my own AMR. Using a laptop motherboard, 2D LiDAR, and wheel encoders, I created a 4-wheel robot running ROS2 Foxy. After months of debugging and calibration, I successfully mapped my university department—a huge step in mastering hardware integration from the ground up.

The Experience: Internships at IIT Bombay & RigBetel Labs

As an intern at IIT Bombay, I worked on the same e-Yantra robot, implementing autonomous docking with ArUco markers and diving into Behavior Trees with `py_trees`. Later, at RigBetel Labs, I specialized in the ROS2 Nav2 stack, developing custom BT plugins and gaining hands-on experience with professional AGVs.

The Professional: Associate Robotics Developer

My successful internship at RigBetel Labs led to a full-time role as an Associate Robotics Developer. Today, I work on a variety of real-world mobile robots and robotic arms, heavily focusing on Nav2 and Behavior Trees to create robust, intelligent, and autonomous systems.
