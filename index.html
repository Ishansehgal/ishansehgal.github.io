My Robotics Journey

First Steps into Robotics

2021 - BTech First Year

Built my first autonomous project - an obstacle avoidance car using Arduino and ultrasonic sensors. Added environmental monitoring with AQI and gas sensors, plus a custom battery pack.

Discovering ROS

2022 - Second Year

Started my ROS journey with Rigbetech Labs mentorship. Learned Fusion 360, created differential drive models, exported to URDF, and implemented ROS Nav stack in ROS Noetic. Explored localization and odometry concepts.

E-Yantra Robotics Competition

Sept - Dec 2023

Competed with autonomous AMR and UR5 robotic arm project. Progressed to Stage 2 with remote access to real robots at IIT Bombay. Achieved AIR 13 rank and learned the gap between simulation and reality.

Building My First Real Robot

Jan 2024

Constructed a 4-wheel differential drive robot with 2D LiDAR, wheel encoders, and repurposed laptop motherboard. Implemented ROS2 Foxy (later Humble) for localization, odometry, and department mapping.

IIT Bombay Research Internship

March - July 2024

Worked on automating robot operations for future teams. Implemented autonomous docking using ArUco markers and developed behavior trees with PyTrees. Gained invaluable hands-on experience with professional robotics systems.

Associate Robotics Developer

July 2024 - Present

Currently working at Rigbetech Labs on diverse robots from small turtlebots to large AGVs and AMRs. Specializing in ROS2 Nav2, behavior trees, and custom plugin development for navigation systems.
