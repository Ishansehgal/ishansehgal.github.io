My Philosophy: Behavior Trees

I view problem-solving through the logic of BTs. My career is a Sequence: 'Learn ROS' → 'Build Projects' → 'Get Job'. Debugging is a Fallback: 'Check Code' → 'Check Hardware' → 'Ask for Help'. This structured approach is key to robust robotics.
